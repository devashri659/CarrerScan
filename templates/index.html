About CareerScan

AI-Powered Job Offer Risk Checker — Final Year Engineering Project.

Project Motivation & Abstract

Online employment scams have escalated drastically, particularly targeting university students, recent graduates, and desperate job seekers. Fraudsters increasingly impersonate reputable multinational corporations, leveraging platforms like Telegram, WhatsApp, and spoofed domains to execute advance-fee fraud, counterfeit check disbursement, and sensitive identity theft.

CareerScan was engineered as an automated decision-support system that inspects job descriptions, recruitment emails, and PDF offer letters to quantify scam probabilities and surface concrete, explainable red flags in real time.

Dual-Engine System Architecture

CareerScan integrates both statistical machine learning and deterministic rule-based verification:

1
Document Ingestion & Normalization: Parses text inputs or extracts unformatted streams from uploaded PDF offer letters using PyPDF.
2
Deterministic Heuristics Engine: Scans 5 core fraud vectors (Fake Check Schemes, Irreversible Payments, Chat-only Interviews, Unrealistic Salaries, Artificial Urgency).
3
Scikit-Learn NLP Classifier: Extracts bi-gram TF-IDF vectors and calculates scam probabilities via a trained Logistic Regression model.
4
Domain & Corporate Impersonation Audit: Cross-references corporate claims against public webmail providers (@gmail, @yahoo) and flags mismatch anomalies.
5
SQLite Persistent Audit Log: Maintains full scan histories for audits, tracking, and trend analysis.

Anatomy of Prevalent Recruitment Scams

The Overpayment & Fake Equipment Check

The victim is mailed a counterfeit cashier's check to purchase home office gear from an 'approved vendor'. The victim transfers real funds via Zelle/Bitcoin, and days later the bank reverses the bad check, leaving the applicant in debt.

The Telegram / Anonymous Chat Interview

Interviews conducted exclusively through Telegram or WhatsApp text without video or verified phone calls. The scammer poses as an HR executive and extends immediate offers without verifying skills.

The Pay-to-Play Internship

Demands a 'refundable training deposit', 'registration fee', or 'server fee' before counter-signing the agreement. Legitimate internships always pay the candidate, never the reverse.

Project Scope & Ethical Disclaimer

CareerScan is designed for educational and informational purposes as a computer engineering capstone project. Risk scores reflect algorithmic probability and pattern correlations. A low-risk score does not constitute a legal guarantee of authenticity; job seekers should always independently verify opportunities directly with company HR portals.
